# Stock Performance After IPO — 2025 & 2026 US IPOs

A static report on how US-listed IPOs from 2025 and 2026 year-to-date have performed
relative to their offer prices, as of June 10, 2026.

**Live report:** https://sooflee.github.io/elekid/

## Highlights

- Only 38.3% of 2025 IPOs were trading above their offer price at year-end 2025
- Winners are concentrated in AI infrastructure (CoreWeave +143%, Cerebras +29%) and stablecoins (Circle +~160%)
- Fintech/BNPL and crypto listings dominate the losers (Klarna −59%, BitGo −70%)
- The Renaissance IPO Index returned +5% in 2025 vs +18% for the S&P 500, then −8% in Q1 2026

## Data sources

Renaissance Capital US IPO market reviews (2025 year-end, 1Q 2026), stockanalysis.com
IPO trackers, TechCrunch, and TipRanks. Prices are intraday snapshots from June 10, 2026.
Full methodology notes and caveats are in the report itself.
